# Tampa Bay Masjids

A masjid link page for the Facebook group "Muslims Community in Tampa Bay".

## Language

**Masjid**:
A place in Tampa Bay listed for congregational prayer, including a recurring Jumu'ah-only location.
_Avoid_: Mosque (in code and data)

**Iqama time**:
The time a masjid sets for the congregational prayer to begin.
_Avoid_: Prayer time (it can also mean the adhan time)

**Jumu'ah time**:
A time a masjid sets for Friday prayer. A masjid may have several.

**Confirmed time**:
An iqama or Jumu'ah time with a known source and the date it was checked. Only confirmed times are shown.
_Avoid_: Current time, latest time

**Source**:
The channel a masjid's time came from, such as its website or a WhatsApp announcement.

**Pinned masjid**:
The masjid a visitor chose to see first.
_Avoid_: Favorite
